Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Lumina Sound for Android, iOS and desktop, developed and published by Marcos Octavio Guerra Liso.

EFFECTIVE DATE · OCTOBER 14, 2026

1. Who We Are

Lumina Sound is a music and podcast player developed by Marcos Octavio Guerra Liso ("we," "us," or "our"), based in Panama. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used, which third parties are involved, and the choices available to you.

Lumina Sound has no user accounts, and we do not run servers that receive your music, listening activity or personal information.

2. Information the App Uses

Your music library. With your permission (media/audio access on Android, Music library access on iOS, or the folders you choose on desktop), the app reads the audio files on your device and their metadata (title, artist, album, artwork) so it can list and play them. This information stays on your device and is never uploaded by us.

Your app data. Playlists, favorites, the playback queue, podcast subscriptions and downloads, lyrics you have loaded, and your settings are stored only on your device, in an encrypted local database.

Microphone permission (Android only). Android requires this permission for the audio visualizer API that animates the visualizer from the music the app itself is playing. The app does not record, store or transmit any sound from your microphone.

Notifications. The app shows a media notification with playback controls while music or podcasts are playing.

Diagnostic logs. The app may keep technical logs on your device to help troubleshoot problems. They are not sent to us automatically.

3. Advertising (free version on Android and iOS)

The free version of Lumina Sound shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or the iOS IDFA only if you allow tracking), your IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads.

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. Where US state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • iOS tracking. On iOS the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized using the IDFA.
  • Premium. Lumina Sound Premium removes ads. While you have Premium, the advertising SDK is not started at all.
  • Desktop. The desktop version shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK.

You can learn how Google uses this information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics (Android and iOS)

On Android and iOS, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Only the events Firebase collects automatically are used, such as app opens, sessions, app updates, and in-app purchases. With them, Firebase receives an app instance identifier, your device model, operating system and app version, language, and approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address.

  • The app does not send your music library, file names, playlists or listening history to Firebase.
  • Analytics follows the consent you give in Google's consent form in the regions where consent applies, and you can change it from Settings → Privacy choices.
  • The desktop version contains no analytics SDK.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase. On Android and iOS, payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and the purchase is verified through RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID. On desktop, Premium is unlocked with a license key sold and validated by Lemon Squeezy. The app sends the key and an activation identifier to Lemon Squeezy to validate it, and stores them on your device. We never receive your payment card details.

6. Other Third-Party Services

Some features contact third-party services directly from your device. These services receive your IP address as part of the request.

  • Lyrics: to find lyrics, the app sends the track's title, artist, album and duration to LRCLIB.
  • Podcasts: podcast search and top charts use Apple's public iTunes Search and podcast chart services, which receive your search terms (Apple Privacy Policy). Podcast feeds and episodes are downloaded from each podcast's own hosting server.
  • Equalizer headphone profiles: when you look up a headphone model for AutoEQ, the app downloads its profile from the public AutoEq project hosted on GitHub (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Scrobbling (optional): if you connect a Last.fm or ListenBrainz account in Settings, the app sends the title, artist, album and time of the tracks you play to that account. Your session key or user token is stored only on your device, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Streams you open: when you play an audio link you enter, the app connects to the server hosting it.
  • Payments, ads and analytics: Google Play, the Apple App Store, RevenueCat, Lemon Squeezy, Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase, as described in this policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use crash-reporting SDKs.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Change your ad and analytics consent at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the app (shown where consent applies).
  • On iOS, change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Grant or revoke media, Music library and microphone permissions from your device's system settings.
  • Disconnect Last.fm or ListenBrainz scrobbling at any time from the app's Settings.
  • Delete all data stored by the app by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, requests about advertising and analytics data are best directed to Google, but you can always contact us and we will help.

8. Children's Privacy

Lumina Sound is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
